[ BEYOND THE BOWL ]

Scientific Name:

Fishy

Discovered by / at:

Echonnie, September 2026

Family:

Technically, the fish tank is its first home. Then there are five buddies on its way to a broader world. (ocean) After that, The ocean become both its home and its enemy. (Hooks will appear as enemies!) Finally, its only by itself, alone but grown-up in the universe.

Habitat:

Anywhere. Grows from the fish tank, to the pond, to the ocean, and finally to the universe.

Appearance:

The goldfish has sparkling eyes, gently trembling fins, and a swaying tail Its color is changing gradually as it grows.

Key Characteristics:

  • Fish tank: can hit the walls.
  • Pond: can shrink.
  • Ocean: can eat "fish food".
  • Universe: can be vast and lonely.

What we know so far about [ Fishy ]? The story follows the Fishy as it first learns about its environment, adapts to its rules and patterns, discovers what makes it different, and eventually breaks through its environment and enters a new one. As the environment becomes larger and less predictable, the visual patterns also become darker, more complex, and more irregular. Fishy's story tells us that growth does not only mean becoming stronger, but also understanding one's environment, finding individuality, and having the courage to move beyond familiar boundaries. Fishy is a metaphor for the human experience, as we all navigate through different environments, face challenges, and grow into our own unique selves!
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Try dragging, pressing the mouse or arrow keysto move and interact with Fishy to explore all four stages! The instructions are shown on the screen. The creature will respond to your actions, and you can observe how it adapts and changes in each environment. Enjoy the journey with Fishy! (There's a easter egg hidden in the universe stage, can you find it? Hint: try spelling "happy"!)
